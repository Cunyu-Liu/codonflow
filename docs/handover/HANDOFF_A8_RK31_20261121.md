## 1. Scientific question and frozen decision rule

RK31 tested whether explicit context-gated MFE effects improve variant-OOD prediction when the same whole sequence clusters are held out in all three known Mordstein contexts. It is a retrospective outcome-known follow-up using the independently accepted RK30 matched-context table, not an independent confirmation, unseen-context transport test, protein-OOD result, functional-untouched evaluation, or external-SOTA result.

Protocol: `configs/a8-rk31-known-context-applicability-v1.json`
Schema: `a8-rk31-known-context-applicability-protocol-v1`
Protocol SHA256: `499dc5f92458c467a18256054cf3f6adba06376f8e2fcf618be5ad903d219bf1`

The six frozen arms were `null`, `shared_simple`, `shared_full`, `context_gated_full`, `no_transport_simple`, and `no_transport_full`. Simple features were GC3, GC, CpG, and CAI. Full features added full-300-nt MFE, 48-nt minimum-window MFE, and window-mean MFE. The gated arm added six context-indicator × MFE interactions; simple slopes remained shared. Per-context models were fitted without transport. Fold-local scaling and `Ridge(alpha=1.0)` were fixed without tuning.

Primary seed: `20261222`; sensitivity seeds: `20261222`–`20261231`; five whole-cluster folds; 10,000 whole-cluster bootstraps (`20261523`) and 10,000 cluster sign-swap permutations (`20261524`). The 24-endpoint Holm family was 3 contexts × 4 baselines × 2 metrics. Every PASS required all four:

1. primary-seed `observed_effect > 0` (not `mean_seed_effect`);
2. 95% whole-cluster CI lower bound > 0;
3. Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across the 24 endpoints;
4. at least 8/10 seed effects positive.

Broad applicability required 24/24. The dedicated gating-over-shared-full gate required its six endpoints. No endpoint or arm was removed after seeing results.
