## 1. Decision

**`MATCHED_CONTEXT_MFE_TRANSPORT_NOT_SUPPORTED`.** None of the 16 primary transport endpoints passed. All four ordered transport directions failed. None of the 12 secondary within-context endpoints passed.

This is a falsification of the frozen claim that adding the three predicted-MFE features gives a broadly context-transportable increment over the fair sequence-only baseline in this matched GFP design. It is **not** a universal claim that MFE has no biological effect in every host or intron context.

RK29 remains `NOT_DISCRIMINATING_SOURCE_ALIGNMENT_OR_COVERAGE_FAILED`: iCodon S6 outcomes could not be uniquely mapped to the GEO perfect-variant sequences. No RK29 analysis rows, effects or mechanism conclusion were created.

## 2. What RK30 discriminates — and what it does not

RK30 holds the Mordstein 2020 GFP study, vector, variants, observed 300-nt R1 fragment and predicted-MFE definition fixed. It separates HeLa no-intron, HeLa with-intron and HEK293 no-intron conditions and evaluates ordered host/intron transport by intact sequence cluster.

Relative to broader RK27/RK28 comparisons, this removes study, vector, variant-set, observed-fragment and MFE-definition differences from the transport contrast. Depth is QC-only, so the simple and structure arms are fair sequence predictors. RK27 measured full-CDS/5′ structure and RK28's earlier depth-containing baseline are not retroactively made equivalent to this experiment.

Still not discriminated: causal host mechanism, measured rather than predicted structure, direct translation velocity/decay, complete-CDS or protein OOD, external SOTA, prospective design-build-test, and generator advantage. Functional untouched data were not read, expensive generator training was not authorized, and RK-G1 remains open.

## 3. Frozen data and counting

- 64 R1 runs: eight existing HEK293 plus 56 new HeLa files; no second download campaign.
- Fixed space: 152 observed-R1 references and 74 sequence clusters; no reclustering or outcome-based variant selection.
- Eight experiments, Bin1–8; unique nearest-reference assignment with maximum Hamming error 8 nt.
- Required complete Bin1–8 and at least 1,000 assigned reads per variant/replicate.
- Common cohort: **126 variants / 69 clusters**; 71,814,214 assigned reads across 9,728 count rows.
- Condition outcomes: HeLa no-intron 4.49844 ± 1.09052; HeLa with-intron 4.44398 ± 1.05183; HEK293 no-intron 5.08069 ± 1.34973.
- Simple: GC3, GC, CpG, CAI. Structure: simple + full-300-nt MFE, 48-nt minimum-window MFE and window-mean MFE.
- Frozen Ridge/HistGB settings; 10 seeds × 5 folds; 10,000 whole-cluster bootstraps and 10,000 whole-cluster sign-swap permutations.
- 800 transport fits + 900 within-context fits = 1,700 fits; CPU-only, one thread.
