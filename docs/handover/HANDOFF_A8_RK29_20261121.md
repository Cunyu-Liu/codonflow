## 2. Scientific question and preregistered decision rule

RK29 asked whether four computational ViennaRNA MFE features add out-of-protein predictive information to a strong iCodon/codon/amino-acid/design baseline for injected-mRNA decay in one-cell zebrafish embryos. The planned contrast was `structure − simple` for Ridge and HistGB, using pooled OOF Spearman and MSE improvement as four confirmatory endpoints.

The frozen protocol required a unique one-to-one mapping between all 955 S6 outcome rows and 955 canonical FASTA names before model fitting. Row order, fuzzy/nearest matching, arbitrary first match, target-informed selection and post-hoc join revision were forbidden. Failed source alignment or coverage had to be classified as not discriminating rather than as a mechanism-negative result.

Frozen design, not executed results:

- 950 variants, 96 proteins and 96 evaluation groups after a label-free minimum-three-variants-per-protein filter.
- iCodon-only, 93-feature simple, 97-feature structure and descriptive MFE-only arms.
- Ridge and HistGB; 10 seeds × 5 folds × 4 arms × 2 backbones = 400 planned fits.
- 10,000 evaluation-group bootstraps and 10,000 sign-swap permutations; Holm correction over four endpoints.
- No tuning or split revision was permitted. None of the 400 fits occurred because the alignment gate stopped production first.
