## 1. Scientific scope and verdict

RK33 asked a deliberately narrow retrospective question on the same Mordstein HEK293 observed-R1 variants, 74 whole-sequence clusters, 1,520 fold assignments, simple features, models and inference used by RK28: does a CDS4–45 local 42-nt ViennaRNA MFE outperform (i) no structure, (ii) a parameter-matched full-300 single-MFE arm, and (iii) RK28's full-300/window-48 three-feature arm?

This is `retrospective_outcome_known_coverage_followup_not_independent_confirmation`. The observed FASTA starts at CDS position 4 and lacks start ATG, the upstream four bases and complete UTR–CDS sequence. RK33 therefore does **not** exactly reproduce the Kudla `−4…37` window and cannot identify host, assay, measured-versus-predicted structure or dangles effects.

Final verdict: **0/12 endpoints passed**. All three families failed: `local42_vs_simple`, `local42_vs_full1`, and `local42_vs_full3`. All 12 confidence intervals crossed zero. The smallest unadjusted permutation p was `0.16799160041997901`; all unified-Holm p values were 1.0. This was not a borderline failure caused only by multiplicity correction. No local-structure advantage is claimed.
